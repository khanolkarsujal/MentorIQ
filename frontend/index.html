The Process

Three Steps. Zero Guesswork.

02

AI Staff Audit

Our AI acts as your on-call Staff Engineer, performing a deep-dive code review across our 5 Technical Maturity Pillars. We evaluate your architectural patterns, not just your line count.

Mentor Match

Receive a professional Maturity Score, specific skill-gap diagnostics, and a mentor archetype precision-matched to bridge the gap between your current level and your career goals.
