The Process

Three Steps. Zero Guesswork.

01

Provide your GitHub username. We automatically pull your latest repos, language distribution, stars, and README content — no forms to fill.

02

Our LLM acts as a Staff Engineer reviewing a pull request. It grades your work across 5 technical maturity pillars unique to real engineering teams.

You receive a Maturity Score, targeted skill-gap diagnosis, and a mentor archetype matched exactly to the gap between where you are and where you need to be.
